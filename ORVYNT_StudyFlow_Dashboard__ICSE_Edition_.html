Welcome to Your Study Flow

ORVYNT StudyFlow is an intuitive dark-themed digital and printable dashboard tailored specifically for ICSE students. Designed to align with the rigor of Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, and History & Civics, this system helps you organize your study hours, track chapter coverage, and execute a 3-pass revision plan.

Customization & ICSE Syllabus Sync

We have pre-loaded standard ICSE chapters. You can customize chapter names, add school test topics, and adjust exam target dates under the ICSE Chapter Tracker and Exam Revision tabs anytime.

1. Main Dashboard

Set 3 Weekly Goals, 3 Daily Priorities, monitor overall Subject Progress, and record a Sunday reflection.

2. Chapter Tracker

Toggle chapters between Not Started, In Progress, and Completed to auto-update progress bars.

3. 3-Pass Revision

Track Pass 1 (Formula Notes), Pass 2 (Concept Numerical), and Pass 3 (ICSE PYQ Past Papers) per subject.

4. Auto-Save & Export

All data auto-saves in your browser. Export to JSON for cross-device backup or print clean A4 paper PDFs.
