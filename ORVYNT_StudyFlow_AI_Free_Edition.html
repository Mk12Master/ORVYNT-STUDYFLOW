Welcome to ORVYNT StudyFlow

Designed for ICSE Class 9 & 10 board aspirants, this digital planner balances structured chapter coverage with high-priority revision loops. Use it smoothly inside Safari or Chrome on your iPad, tablet, or laptop.

1. Main Dashboard

Tracks top weekly goals, 3 daily priorities, live ICSE progress meters, and weekly reflections.

2. Daily Study Planner

Structured hourly study blocks, target must-wins, homework submission log, and hydration counter.

3. Weekly Timetable

Structure fixed study routines for Monday through Sunday across morning, afternoon, and night slots.

4. Chapter Tracker

Preloaded ICSE core chapters with tri-state status badges (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
